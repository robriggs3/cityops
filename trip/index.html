Compares against your "Estimated total cost" averaged per month. My intent is that each city's estimated cost should reflect city spend for housing only, not flights, transit, or other travel logistics between cities. I recommend you track those separately in your own budget. This isn't an expense tracker; it's a planning sanity check for where you want to be.
